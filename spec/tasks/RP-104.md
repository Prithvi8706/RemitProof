## 1. Objective and rationale

After the investigator returns a proposal, verify it against the proof bundle and the search's alternatives, not against the capped prompt bundle. The model keeps receiving the prompt bundle.

Why: finding F3, proposal half. Today a proposal for the one allocation visible in the capped bundle resolves even when a second allocation exists among the dropped invoices. After this task the gate sees the second allocation and reports ambiguity.

This also completes DD-05: from here on `process_payment` enumerates in exactly one place, `search`.

Investigators:
