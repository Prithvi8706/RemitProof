## 1. Objective and rationale

Remove two rules from the sufficiency gate that judge how the proposal cites, not whether the allocation is supported:

1. A selected credit must be repeated in `evidence_ids`.
2. An email counts for disambiguation only if the proposal cited it.

Why: DD-08. Three of the nine published false escalations (`PAY_057`, `PAY_063`, `PAY_068`) proposed exactly the correct allocation and were refused only by rule 1. The protection these rules seem to offer is already provided elsewhere, for cited and uncited emails alike: the proof engine checks every candidate email of the proposed customer for contradictions (`backend/app/services/proof_engine.py:353`), and only emails from a trusted sender, of the proposed customer, and not superseded can disambiguate.

What stays a gate: an empty citation list, a cited ID that is not in the bundle, and the semantic-claim citation checks. Tests 6 and 7 pin them.

## 3. Current behaviour and references

Rule 1 is the block at `backend/app/services/evidence_sufficiency.py:48` to `:53` inside `_proposal_evidence_issues`: three comment lines, then a loop that appends each selected credit ID that is not in `cited_ids` to `issues`. An issue becomes `missing_required_evidence`, which blocks resolution.

Rule 2 is the first condition of the `if` at `backend/app/services/evidence_sufficiency.py:101` to `:106` inside `_evidence_disambiguates`: `email.email_id not in cited_ids` (`:102`). The local `cited_ids` in that function is assigned at `backend/app/services/evidence_sufficiency.py:88` and used nowhere else in the function.

In `_proposal_evidence_issues`, `cited_ids` (`:32`) is still used by the semantic-claim check at `:45` and is returned. Keep it there.

Three existing tests assert the old behaviour: `test_uncited_email_cannot_disambiguate_alternatives`, `test_selected_credit_record_cannot_disambiguate_competing_credits` and `test_selected_credit_must_be_cited`, all in `backend/tests/test_evidence_integrity.py`.
